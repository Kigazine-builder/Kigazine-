How the cycle works

  1. Choose one subject or add your own.
  2. Edit your section and save drafts anytime.
  3. Hand it in by the Wednesday deadline.
  4. Apple Times collects sections every two weeks.
  5. Unfinished drafts get an AI completion labelled for editor review.
  6. The editor desk assembles the next issue for publication.
