How publishing works

1. Write
Create a draft without sharing private information.
2. Submit
Your work enters the assigned adult’s private queue.
3. Review
The moderator approves, rejects, or requests changes.
4. Read
Only approved work appears in the class feed.

Never include

🚫 Full names or personal email addresses
🚫 Phone numbers, home addresses, or passwords
🚫 Private photos, school badges, or schedules
🚫 Bullying, dangerous challenges, or contact invitations
