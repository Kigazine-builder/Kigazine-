Quick safety tips

  • Share kind ideas Stories, reviews, and clubs work best when they sound friendly and welcoming.
  • Keep private things private Leave out real names, schools, addresses, passwords, and contact details.
  • Ask a trusted grown-up Before sharing online, check with a parent, guardian, or teacher.

Before you share

Kigazine is for creative ideas, stories, clubs, reviews, and learning. Posts should not include private information or anything mean, scary, unsafe, or inappropriate for kids.

  • Use a nickname, not your full real name.
  • Do not post your address, school, phone number, email, passwords, or exact location.
  • Ask a trusted grown-up before sharing anything online.
