🎮 Blockcade

A local, single-file arcade map designer and test player. Design levels, validate them, save them in your browser, export portable JSON, then play them in the arcade.

Editor

Brush, fill, rectangle, picker, undo/redo, mirroring, autosave, and saved levels.

Gameplay

Coins, spikes, lava, keys, locks, portals, ice, checkpoints, deaths, moves, keyboard controls, and mobile buttons.

Exports

Versioned JSON with width, height, timestamps, tile metadata, validation, and backward-compatible import.
